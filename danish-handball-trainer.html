Pronunciation traps (read once)
  • Soft D: a "d" after a vowel is a soft "th" as in "the": med, rød, blad.
  • æ, ø, å: æ ≈ short "air", ø ≈ "uh" with rounded lips, å ≈ "aw".
  • Swallowed endings: -et and -ede almost vanish: spillede ≈ "spell-uh".
  • Stød: a throat catch that changes meaning (hun "she" vs hund "dog"). Learn by ear.
  • jeg = "yai". hv- = "v". Final g is often silent.
  • Rhythm is flat and fast. Coaches use short fragments: master the shouts and positions first.
